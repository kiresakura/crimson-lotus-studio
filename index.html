What we build

Last Glow (最後的餘暉) — a multiplayer card game set in Victorian Britain. Players live through the Industrial Revolution and feel how it reshaped every layer of society — the same kind of shock we are facing again with AI today.

About

Crimson Lotus Studio is an independent project founded by a history undergraduate at National Cheng Kung University, Taiwan. We combine historical research with game design to turn the past into something people can experience, not just read about.

How we work

  • Historical events on cards checked against sources before they reach the table.
  • Play-tests and community events planned with students.
